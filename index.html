Kit proposal · Maxime

Three kits, each one a complete training session

Instead of loose accessories, every kit lets the buyer do a real workout the day it arrives. One product per function, a variant for each body and need, and add-ons on top. All three are designed like lifestyle objects people want to film and keep on display.

One function, one productNo two items doing the same job. Duplicates become variants the buyer picks.
Variants by needHands that blister, weak grip, heavy lifts: each problem gets its own version.
Bundles carry the weak itemsSweatbands, sliders and tubes lose money alone. In one parcel with a hero they become profit.
Heavy items stay optionalAb roller, pull-up bar and big bag go as add-ons or US-warehouse items so the base parcel stays light.
